# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Participation in Franchise Management should be respectful, constructive, and welcoming to people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient.
- Assume good intent while addressing impact clearly.
- Give and receive actionable feedback.
- Focus on the work and its effect, not personal attributes.
- Protect private information and use the security process for vulnerabilities.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, sexualized conduct, doxxing, deliberate disruption, and publishing private or security-sensitive information without authorization are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the repository's private contact channel. Do not use a public issue for sensitive reports. Reports will be reviewed fairly and handled as confidentially as practical. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other appropriate action.
